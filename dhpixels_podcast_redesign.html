Podcast FAQ

Common questions businesses ask before starting a podcast, especially when they need video, audio, studio help, and edited content.

Do I need my own studio?

No. We can help find a Chicago studio or build around a location you already have.

Can this be video and audio?

Yes. The strongest business podcasts usually capture both.

How many cameras do I need?

Most shows work well with two or three cameras. Four cameras can help for multiple guests.

What if I do not know the format yet?

That is part of the process. We can help shape the structure, length, guest style, intro, and outro.

Can you make clips?

Yes. Short clips are one of the main reasons to record a business podcast.

How do we start?

Call, text, or email with the type of show you want to make and whether you already have a location.
