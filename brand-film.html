Brand Film Reside Home Loans

Building trust through story.

A cinematic brand film created for Reside Home Loans in Wicker Park, built to show the people behind the company and make mortgage content feel real, polished, and human.

ClientReside Home Loans
CategoryBrand Film
LocationWicker Park, Chicago
StyleCommercial Documentary
Watch the Film Start a Project
Final Film

More than a mortgage company.

This was not built to feel like a generic corporate promo. The goal was to show the people, the culture, the energy, and the trust behind Reside Home Loans through a clean, cinematic brand film.

The Point

Make the company feel human before the first call.

The Need

In finance, trust is everything. A strong brand film gives people a reason to believe in the team before they ever step into a meeting.

The Result

A polished, interview-driven piece that feels confident, personal, and useful across websites, social media, recruiting, and client outreach.

The Assignment

A brand film with a documentary pulse.

Reside needed something that felt elevated without losing the personal side of the business. We shaped the piece around honest interviews, real office energy, strong visuals, and the feeling of being welcomed into the team.

01 / Strategy

Built before shoot day.

We planned the structure, interview direction, visual style, and overall story before production so the final piece had a clear purpose.

02 / Production

Natural light mixed with control.

The office gave us great natural light, then we shaped it with additional lighting to keep the look clean, dimensional, and commercial.

03 / Story

Real people first.

The film was driven by real team conversations, not stiff corporate lines. That is what makes the piece feel honest and believable.

Why It Works

Financial content does not have to feel cold.

Mortgage content can easily become flat, scripted, and forgettable. This film went the other direction. The look stayed polished, but the message stayed human.

That is the value of a brand film. It gives your company a voice, a face, and a reason for people to trust you before they ever become a client.

Production Approach

Every choice protects the story.

The film needed to look premium, but still feel real. We used interviews, office b-roll, natural light, shaped light, clean audio, and a strong edit to create a brand piece that feels intentional from start to finish.

Pre-Production

Creative planning, interview direction, story structure, and a clear production roadmap.

Lighting

Natural window light mixed with controlled lighting to create a clean, dimensional look.

Audio

Clean dialogue capture built to make each interview feel clear, direct, and professional.

Editing

Interview-led structure, polished pacing, office b-roll, sound, color, and brand rhythm.

The Result

Less like a commercial. More like the identity of a team.

The final piece gave Reside Home Loans something stronger than a basic promo. It gave them a cinematic brand asset that can build trust, show culture, and make the company feel real.

Gallery

Wicker Park, windows, and real conversations.

A mix of team moments, production stills, office visuals, and video frames that show how brand storytelling can feel polished without feeling fake.

For Companies

Your team is already the story.

Most companies do not need louder marketing. They need clearer storytelling. A strong brand film gives people a reason to care, a reason to trust you, and a reason to remember you.
